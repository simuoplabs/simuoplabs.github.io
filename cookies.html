Cookie Policy

SimUoPLabs does not use analytics, advertising, social-media pixels or other non-essential tracking cookies on this website.

Preference storage

When you actively change the language or an accessibility setting, the site stores that preference in your browser’s local storage so it can be remembered on a later visit. This storage is used only for the feature you selected. It is not used for advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking.

Third-party requests

The site requests fonts from Google Fonts. The contact form uses FormSubmit only after you submit it. These services may process technical information such as an IP address under their own policies. Social sites are linked, not embedded.

Your choices

You can remove local storage and cookies at any time through your browser’s site-data controls. Removing site data resets language and accessibility preferences. Because the site does not deploy non-essential cookies, no cookie banner or tracking-consent choice is required for normal browsing.

If analytics, advertising, an embedded video, or another non-essential third-party service is added later, this policy and the consent mechanism must be updated before that service is activated.
