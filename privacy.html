Privacy Policy

This notice explains how SimUoPLabs handles personal data submitted through this website. It applies to this website only, not to external sites linked from it.

Who is responsible

SimUoPLabs is responsible for the information collected through this website. For privacy questions or requests, email simuoplabs@gmail.com.

Data we collect

We collect only the name, email address and message that you choose to send through the contact form. Please do not include special-category or otherwise sensitive personal data in the form.

Why we use it

We use submitted information only to respond to the enquiry and manage related communication. The form asks for an affirmative consent before it can be sent. You may withdraw that consent by emailing us.

Service providers and external links

Form submissions are delivered using FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery provider. Google Fonts are requested to display the site typefaces. Social-media links are ordinary links, not embeds; their services receive information only after you choose to visit them. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels or session-replay tools.

Retention and security

Messages are retained only for as long as needed to respond and manage the enquiry, then deleted or anonymised, normally within 12 months unless a longer period is legally required. Access is limited to people handling the enquiry.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, and you may withdraw consent. You may also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

This policy is an operational website notice, not legal advice. The organisation should have a qualified Greek privacy professional review it before collecting personal data at scale.
