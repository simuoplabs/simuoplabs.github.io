# SimUoPLabs Backlink Strategy

## Goal

Earn relevant, editorial links that reinforce SimUoPLabs as a student engineering research team in Patras. Prioritize relevance and genuine collaboration over directory volume.

## First 90 days

1. Ask the University of the Peloponnese department and student-activity pages to link to the home page from the team profile, research-news and competition pages.
2. Ask each named sponsor to link to the sponsor page from its community, CSR or partnerships page. Provide the precise page URL and a short project description.
3. Publish a short result page after each competition, workshop or research milestone, then share it with the host organization, faculty partners and local STEM media for attribution links.
4. Create profiles on credible engineering and student-competition directories only where a profile is editorially reviewed or actively used by the community.
5. Give partner schools a ready-to-use paragraph and the exact URL for STEM workshop announcements.

## Link targets and anchors

Use natural anchors such as “SimUoPLabs,” “SimUoPLabs research team,” “student engineering team in Patras,” and the specific project name. Link general mentions to the home page; sponsor acknowledgements to the sponsor page; project coverage to the most relevant future project page.

## Measurement

Track referring domains, linked-page traffic, branded searches and event enquiries monthly in Google Search Console and analytics. Remove or disavow nothing solely because a site has a low metric; evaluate relevance and spam signals first.
